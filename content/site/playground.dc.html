Why the Bat?

Apart from black being my favourite colour, here is the real reason for the metaphor.

A bat navigates places it cannot see. It sends out a signal, listens to what comes back, and builds a picture from the response. It does not guess or wait for the lights to come on.

That is how I design. In startups, I often work with incomplete information: no requirements, few users, or little competitive context. Instead of guessing, I work in short cycles: research, design, collaborate, test, listen, and iterate. I use what comes back from real users to continuously refine the product.
